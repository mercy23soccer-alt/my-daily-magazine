1. **“Degenerative Strain‑Enabled Multicomponent Assembly for Chiral Pyrrolidine Derivatives”**  
   *Murata, K.; Tsuji, M.; Tokuyama, A.* – *Organic Process Research & Development* (OPRD) – DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.oprd.2026.00431>  
   *Core:* The authors introduce a strain‑relief strategy that drives a rapid, single‑pot multicomponent reaction creating bulky chiral pyrrolidines. A modular design bypasses protecting groups and uses a phosphonium salt as a latent catalyst.  
   *Substrate scope:* Amides, aryl halides, and heteroaryl ketones are tolerated; functional groups such as esters, nitriles, and ethers survive unscathed.  
   *Process view:* The reaction runs at ambient pressure, 60 °C, and delivers 98 % yield with 90 % ee
